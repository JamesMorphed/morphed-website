02 — Page load

The first breath

A 900ms curtain, a staggered hero, and a wake-up on the nav. Total cost: ~1.2 seconds of choreographed silence before the page is fully usable. Refresh to see it again.

0–500ms

Mark settles

Logo fades up, a teal ring pulses out. Sets the tone — alive, but precise.

500–900ms

Curtain lifts

The black veil slides up, revealing the hero. A translateY(-101%), nothing else.

900–1400ms

Hero stagger

Eyebrow, title, lead, metadata — each offset by 100ms. Reads like a typographic cascade.

1400ms+

Nav wakes

Topbar slides down last. The teal status dot begins its 2.4s breath loop.

04 — Scroll transitions

Sections that breathe in

Seven patterns, one IntersectionObserver. Every pattern runs once, in one direction, and respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Pattern 01rise

Rise & fade

The default. 40px upward shift and opacity 0 → 1 over 620ms with ease-out-expo. Works for headlines, paragraphs, cards — anything that enters the viewport once.

data-reveal="up"
Pattern 02slide in from left

Directional slide

For editorial layouts with alternating sides. Content slides in from the direction opposite its text alignment — reinforces reading flow.

data-reveal="left"
Pattern 03slide in from right

Right-side entry

The mirror. Used sparingly — one per section max — to avoid the page feeling like a carousel of passing cars.

data-reveal="right"
Pattern 04scale up

Bloom

Starts at 94% and grows to 100%. Reserved for imagery and hero figures — gives photos and cases a subtle sense of arrival.

data-reveal="scale"
Pattern 05blur sharpen

Sharpen

From 10px blur to zero. Very lightweight — uses only filter on the GPU. Feels cinematic; use once per page, tops.

data-reveal="blur"
Pattern 06wipe reveal

Wipe

A clip-path inset that opens left-to-right. Feels like a headline being written. Pairs well with large typography.

data-reveal="mask"
Pattern 07child stagger

Staggered children

Set data-reveal-children on the parent and --i on each child. Each child is offset by 60ms, producing a soft cascade.

Use for lists, nav columns, card grids, or any run of related items.

data-reveal-children

06 — Principles

Four rules

The why behind the how.

01

Purposeful, never decorative

Every animation explains a state change — entering, loading, focusing, confirming.

02

Once, not always

Reveals fire the first time an element enters view. No repeats on scroll back.

03

Teal is reserved

Glow and ring are brand moments — primary actions, success states, the nav dot.

04

Reduced motion wins

Everything degrades to a 1ms fade. The site is still legible with motion off.
