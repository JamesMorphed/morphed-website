Fast, conversion-led websites designed and built end-to-end in Webflow. One person, one set of files, from wireframe to launch.

The stack, and why

  • Relume to map structure and wireframes in hours, not weeks — reclaim that time for actual design
  • Figma for high-fidelity design and component systems
  • Webflow for build — fast load, clean markup, a CMS your marketing team can actually use
  • GSAP for the moments that need motion — hero entrances, scroll-linked narrative

05 — Process

How a project actually runs.

Same spine on every engagement — scaled up or down to the brief.

01 · Week 1
Diagnose

Immersion, audit, competitor pulse. Before a single pixel — what problem are we actually solving?

02 · Weeks 2–3
Define

Strategy, positioning, IA. A written document that everyone signs before we start making things.

03 · Weeks 3–6
Design

Sketchbook → Figma → high-fidelity. Two rounds of review, one signoff moment.

04 · Weeks 6–10
Deploy

Webflow build, QA, launch. Handover docs and a clean set of files.

07 — Questions

The ones that come up most.

Do you really do everything yourself?

Yes — that's the point of a one-person studio. For projects that genuinely need more hands (a complex illustration system, backend work, motion-heavy campaigns), I bring in a small network of trusted collaborators, always with credit and always with your sign-off.

What's the minimum engagement?

A half-day audit or portfolio review for individual designers. For project work, typically 4 weeks is the smallest useful window — anything less and you're paying for context-switching more than design.

Do you sign NDAs?

Routinely. Several of the engagements I'm proudest of will never appear on this site because of it.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes — about a third of my freelance time is white-label work behind other agencies, both digital and traditional. I'm comfortable in that role and understand the dynamics.

Are you "an AI designer"?

I'm a designer who uses AI. Midjourney is part of the image toolkit; Claude/ChatGPT are part of the research and writing toolkit. The design thinking is still mine — AI is a leverage multiplier, not a co-pilot.

Where are you based, and does it matter?

Margate, Kent. Most of my client work is fully remote — my team at IGT spanned Poland, India, America, and the UK, so distributed collaboration is second nature. In-person days in London or wherever needed are straightforward.
